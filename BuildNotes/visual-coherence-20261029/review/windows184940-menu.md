## Result and unresolved design concerns

No confirmed clipped label, overlapping control, hidden fixed action, stripe motif or blanket artwork tint was found in these13 screenshots at1280×720. Cyan selection treatments remain distinct from pale labels and dark surfaces. Original colour swatches, avatar colours, item art and Shop outfit thumbnails remain visually varied; screenshots alone cannot prove material colour identity byte-for-byte or all lighting states.

Two concrete presentation refinements remain: hide or recompose the avatar while short settings panels are open so its lower body is not isolated below the panel; reduce the unselected Shop’s unused lower area or provide a compact preview state. Both should be addressed coherently with overall layout, not through ad-hoc per-page palette changes.

The bright, richly textured room background has much more detail and different apparent material realism than the simple avatar, item art and charcoal panels. The new neutral UI makes controls legible, but does not resolve that world/UI art-direction mismatch. Current map images were outside this lane’s scope, so map consistency is not independently approved here. Owner acceptance must remain open.
