## Findings that matter for the owner’s request

1. **Detailed-map/art-direction work remains unfinished.** The actual starting rooms are large simple teal shells with wood floors. Parcel Hall adds recognisable original tables/stools/sofa and a better immediate pickup cue, but its captured shape and palette remain nearly identical to Lost Address. Additional decoration should establish a specific place and gameplay route, not just increase scattered object count. The high-detail sunroom lobby is visibly different in realism and density.
2. **Actual map previews are not delivered in these images.** Every Solo map card uses BOX/TABLE fallback object artwork. A place-specific course image would help players distinguish what they are choosing. This report does not diagnose whether absent saved references, capture availability or another cause explains the fallback.
3. **Workshop is technically legible but still too text-heavy and distant.** Original picker art and reduced context labels are improvements. The central overview renders props too small to judge; broad flat floor geometry remains useful for editing but is not detailed-room presentation. A focused selected-room/object view and compact/collapsed seed/settings controls would make direct manipulation easier to understand without hiding the whole floor behind walls. Raw placement and puzzle IDs should stay in advanced details, with meaningful creator labels/pickers in the main flow.
4. **Equipment menu fits; disabled Craft could remain more readable.** Missing-letter information explains why crafting is unavailable, yet the disabled label is almost lost against its surface. Main list/detail/action layout shows no confirmed overlap. The gold in-game HUD/scrollbar and white selected underline differ from cyan lobby selections; coherence should be resolved through shared semantic roles, not independent recolouring of art.
5. **First-action guidance is uneven.** Parcel Hall has visible nearby furniture and a specific E pickup prompt. Lost Address begins with Smash furniture and Bring keepsake while those next targets are not obvious in the starting camera view. A first useful object/route cue is more helpful than adding instruction paragraphs.

No confirmed blocking control overlap or camera penetration was observed in these21 captured views. That narrow finding does not establish overall geometry correctness, navigability or satisfactory visual quality.
