# Public FFA scored-finish plan review

STATUS: IN PROGRESS — source/design review only; no fixture implementation, compilation, network or native execution by this lane.

Parent requested one bounded two-client public FFA scored finish, retaining the nine existing public entry cases, exact approved catalogue, signed whole-party admission, real input/craft/Attack, natural ten-point result and durable ACK. Online is preparing the final plan in `BuildNotes/online/PUBLIC-FFA-SCORED-FINISH-PLAN.md`; this report records source-confirmed constraints before its final review.

Confirmed against current integration: public Run starts its eight-minute cancellation lifetime before allocation and has a ten-minute NUnit limit; these are not new full-game time budgets. FFA's real objective is ten knockouts, with a separate 480-second canonical timeout branch. HAMMER has 28 damage, 30 durability, 1.25 m reach and a 30+8+39 tick attack cycle under current content. At 100 HP with no defense, four eligible hits are needed per knockout. Weapon durability decreases once at the active interval's start even when protection rejects damage. Two actual crafted hammers can therefore cover 40 damaging attacks plus one deliberately protected attack, provided selection switches before a worn weapon becomes unusable and every expected hit is actually observed.

Current non-race RecoveryPosition returns the player's bound Start spawn, not the arena's separately authored Respawn spawn. Ten knockouts permit nine completed respawns: the tenth creates the result and stops canonical Advance. The scorer can remain at a clear point near the victim's actual bound Start; only the still-zero-score victim needs later FullWalk calls. This preserves FullWalk's existing zero-knockout postcondition and avoids changing a helper already used by compact TimeRun/Freestyle.

Two concrete observer requirements were sent to Online. First, the final result makes AuthoritativeMatch.IsPaused true, and Synchronize→SetPermissions clears Attack on that result frame. The final swing cannot wait for a later cycle-end tick or tenth respawn; retain the earlier admitted attack start/weapon and require the natural terminal health/score/result transition in its active interval, then await durable confirmation. Second, a lethal published frame already marks the victim inactive/eliminated. A reused opportunity helper requiring a living target cannot prove that sample. Keep pre-hit eligibility separate from post-hit exact geometry, health/score and hit-ledger evidence, while observing actual native/canonical positions and LOS without altering them.

Route distance, knockback recovery, protection-window command timing and allocation latency still require an explicit conservative plan and native measurement. Per-leg 35-second bounds do not guarantee the whole test fits eight minutes. No timeout extension, injected damage/score/result, teleports or production change is approved by this review.
