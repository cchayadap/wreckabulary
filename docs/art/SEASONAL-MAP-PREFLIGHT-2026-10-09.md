## Constraints to preserve

- **Physics/ownership:** first-pass seasonal dressing has no Collider, Rigidbody, Smashable, LetterBuilt, SummonedThing or loot ownership. Keep original floor/door/stair collision, furniture and letter availability untouched. Non-solid visuals still must stay out of walking routes; avoid making the player visibly pass through a tree. Respect spawn clearances and existing furniture bounds when selecting placement.
- **Visibility:** current cutaways temporarily change renderer state for the requesting camera, then restore it. They include a GPU Resident Drawer opt-out only for affected renderers. Preserve that path and artist-hidden states; do not permanently disable roofs or resize authored walls. Keep downward couch visibility, upward interior ceilings and independent secondary/Scene cameras. Wreaths/garlands that move with a lifted wall belong to that wall's marker.
- **Workshop camera:** simply cloning tall authored geometry is insufficient. `CreativeWorkshop.BeginTour` supplies a dedicated StoreyCutaway, but editor framing currently has no persistent overview-cutaway owner, while `SetTallWalls` changes mesh/transforms. A bridge must provide explicit editing/tour camera visibility and avoid applying that generated-wall mutation to authored geometry. Test returning to editing as well as entering the tour.
- **Budget:** reuse persistent meshes/materials and bounded static groups; no per-frame object/material creation. Keep the proposed room cluster below 6,000 added triangles and 12 renderer submissions before measuring. Use emissive bulb art for a garland; add at most one unshadowed `PracticalLight` if it materially improves the room. Existing profile budgets are Low 0, Medium at most 2, High default 4, Ultra at most 8; player distance is shared across local seats.
- **Surfaces:** preserve floor height, roof/stair holes and 2.4 m doorway clearance. New floor/ceiling variety should be saved material variants or additive trim, not regenerated collision geometry. Avoid overlapping rugs or coplanar decals. The existing stylized window pane is opaque; a snowy window picture must not be described as a real outside view or a new traversable opening.
